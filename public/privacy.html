Data & privacy

Updated October 1, 2026. Questions and requests: info@transcendencemedia.com at Transcendence Media.

Your project records

Projects saved to Google are kept in the Google Sheets workbook you select. Photos and voice memos sent to Google are kept in your Google Drive. StreamLion requests access to files you create with the app or choose for it. Google controls your account and its service terms.

Records on your device

Unfinished drafts, device-only projects, waiting field records and original field files are kept in your browser. An optional workbook copy lets you read projects offline. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to see these records. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; keep a device backup in a safe place.

Google sign-in

With persistent sign-in enabled, StreamLion stores your Google account identifier, email, selected workbook and Drive folder IDs and encrypted Google access and refresh tokens on Cloudflare. A secure browser cookie identifies this device's session. These tokens allow StreamLion to reopen your workbook without a new sign-in prompt. A session lasts up to 90 days, subject to Google revocation and authorization limits. Without persistent sign-in, access tokens remain in browser memory until they expire or the app closes.

Expired server sessions are removed by the daily cleanup service after persistent sign-in is activated. A session that has expired cannot be used while waiting for cleanup.

Disconnecting and removing data

Disconnect in Connections removes this device's stored sign-in session. It keeps your device drafts and your Google records. Other devices have their own sessions. You can also revoke StreamLion access from your Google Account. Delete unwanted Google files in Sheets or Drive. Remove the offline workbook copy in Connections and clear this site's browser storage to remove device records; download and check a backup first. Contact us for help removing server-side sign-in records associated with your account.

ChatGPT and support

Ask lets you copy a dated project snapshot and paste it into your own ChatGPT account. You choose what to share; ChatGPT processes it under its own terms and settings. StreamLion does not pay for a model API or automatically send project text to a model. Downloaded support details contain the app version, browser, connection status and record counts, without project text, workbook IDs or tokens. You decide whether to send that file to support.

Service providers and operational logs

Cloudflare hosts the app and sign-in service; Google provides Sheets, Drive and authorization. These providers may process ordinary connection and request information, such as IP addresses and request times, to operate their services. This release includes no advertising tracker or automatic third-party analytics SDK. StreamLion does not sell your project records. Avoid sending passwords or Google credentials in support emails.
