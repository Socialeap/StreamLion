1. Information we process

  • Project information: details you enter or import, such as names, site addresses, client and contact details, schedules, scope, fees, measurements, field observations and file references. Photos and audio are processed when you choose to record or attach them.
  • Google connection information: your Google account identifier and email, the selected workbook and workspace folder identifiers, and authorization tokens used to access files you create with StreamLion or explicitly select.
  • Retired ChatGPT extension: the customer handoff and plugin/extension routes are no longer available. Existing connection metadata and encrypted temporary review copies remain subject to expiry cleanup; saved project records remain in Google.
  • Purchase information: your Google account identifier and checkout email, Stripe order and payment identifiers, purchased price, payment environment, payment and refund status, and purchase times. These records restore access and handle payments and disputes. StreamLion does not receive or store your full card number or security code. If you choose to save a card for credit top-ups, Stripe keeps it; StreamLion stores only the Stripe customer and card identifiers, the card brand, its last four digits and expiry, until you remove it. Business checkout also records the business-use policy attached to an order. Stripe collects your legal business name and any business tax ID you supply, and may check the ID with tax authorities. We use those details and, when necessary, an authorized private evidence review to determine applicable tax treatment and resolve invoices or pending fulfillment. Do not enter personal identity documents in a business tax field. Business names and tax IDs are not professional-credential verification.
  • Device information: drafts, preferences, device-only records, pending field records, local media and an optional offline workbook copy in your browser.
  • Operational information: connection and request metadata needed to deliver and protect the service. Cloudflare and Google may process IP addresses, request times and other service metadata. StreamLion keeps short-lived request counters tied to your account and a protected hash of an IP address for sign-in rate limits.
  • Optional client coordination and alerts: verified provider/client email, job-scoped access grants, encrypted temporary changes and notification queues, delivery receipt metadata, and encrypted browser push subscriptions. Authoritative job revisions remain in the provider’s Google files.
  • Messages you send us: your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send for support or launch news.

The landing-page examples use sample data. Interacting with them does not read or modify your Google files. The launch-email button prepares a message in your email app; it does not send the message or subscribe you automatically. Checkout is available only when enabled by the app owner. Stripe hosts payment entry and handles payment details. Test checkout is clearly labelled and does not take a real payment.

2. How we use and share information

We process information to open and save your projects, organize field records and measurements, restore your selected workspace, protect sign-in, resolve support requests and operate the app. We use launch-interest messages to respond about StreamLion; you can ask us to stop these messages.

Cloudflare hosts StreamLion and its connection service. With persistent Google sign-in, project content and attachments pass through that service when the app reads or writes Google files. The connection database stores connection information, request counters and, for the retired ChatGPT extension, any remaining encrypted temporary review copies pending expiry cleanup. It is not a separate authoritative project database. Google stores the files you save to your selected Sheets workbook and Drive. Each provider processes information under its own service terms and privacy practices.

We do not sell personal information, use Google data for advertising, or use your project content to train a general-purpose AI model. This release has no advertising tracker or automatic third-party analytics SDK. We may disclose information where necessary to comply with law or protect the service and its users. Any transfer of Google data remains subject to the Google policy described below.

Stripe processes checkout and payment information under its privacy policy. We send a checkout email and an order reference to Stripe; project content and Google authorization tokens are not sent to Stripe. Cloudflare stores the account-linked purchase record. Essential purchase verification does not require an advertising tracker.

When client coordination is enabled, Resend processes transactional email recipients, minimal project/access notices and delivery metadata. Emails link back to the protected portal; they do not contain whole briefs or entry codes. Optional device alerts use your browser/device push service (such as Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft), which receives routing information and encrypted neutral alerts. You enable these alerts explicitly and can turn them off in the project or device settings. Delivery does not establish reading or approval. No marketing subscription is created by a project invitation.

Project sharing permissions remain under your Google account’s control. We do not make your workbook public or enroll you in the forthcoming Providers directory automatically.

3. Google access and security

You authorize Google access on Google’s sign-in screen. StreamLion requests your account identity and email for persistent sign-in, plus access to files you create with the app or choose for it. It does not request unrestricted access to every file in your Drive.

When persistent sign-in is enabled, encrypted access and refresh tokens, your account identifier, email and selected workbook and folder identifiers are stored in Cloudflare’s database. The app uses these to reopen the workspace and send the Google operations you request. Google tokens are encrypted at rest, and connections use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed completely secure.

Optional client coordination requires a separate provider grant for the selected private workbook and Drive folder. It authorizes client updates, notifications and archives for up to one year, including while the provider's browser is closed. The connection service retains encrypted Google credentials for that grant separately from the browser sign-in session. Invited clients verify their email and receive access only to their job's client-visible records and shared files, not the provider's other jobs or private field records. The service also checks the provider's current Core purchase before granting server access.

StreamLion’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used for the visible workspace features you authorize. Human access to Google data is limited to circumstances permitted by that policy, such as your explicit agreement for support, security investigation or legal requirements.

See Google’s privacy policy and Cloudflare’s privacy policy for their practices. These providers may process information in countries other than yours.

4. Browser storage and essential cookies

Your browser stores unfinished drafts, device-only projects, pending uploads, original local field files and, if you enable it, an offline workbook copy. The app also caches its interface for offline use. Someone using the same browser profile may be able to access these records.

Client work-order edits are saved as drafts on this device and are shared with the other party only when you save the current information. Draft restoration is limited to seven days after the last edit; these drafts are not included in the Core device backup. Client sign-out removes local client drafts when browser storage is available and warns when it cannot clear them. Use a private browser profile for client information and keep downloaded work orders in protected storage.

The owner admin portal keeps private reports in memory and clears them on sign-out or admin-session expiry. To avoid repeating a credit adjustment after a lost response, it temporarily stores only an opaque operation identifier and test/live mode in this tab’s session storage until the result is checked. That marker contains no recipient, credit amount, reason or sign-in credential.

Persistent sign-in uses a secure session cookie to identify this browser and a temporary sign-in cookie to complete Google authorization. They are essential connection cookies, not advertising cookies. The sign-in cookie lasts up to ten minutes and the session cookie up to 90 days. Without persistent sign-in, Google access tokens remain in browser memory until they expire or the app closes.

Core workspace sign-out ends only this browser’s session and keeps device records and drafts, other devices and background coordination grants. An encrypted essential workspace cookie remembers the selected workbook and folder for up to 90 days; it cannot sign you in or authorize record access. StreamLion uses it only after Google verifies the same account. Disconnect Google is a separate action that also revokes background coordination access. Clearing cookies removes the bookmark.

Clearing site storage removes browser records and settings, but does not delete files already saved to Google. Browser storage can also be evicted by your device. Download and check a backup before clearing it.

5. Voice, AI and support

Recording a voice memo requires microphone permission. Audio stays in local browser storage unless you choose a Google save that includes it. Keyboard or device dictation is provided by your device or keyboard provider; its own settings determine whether speech is processed on the device or remotely.

The customer ChatGPT handoff and plugin/extension routes have been retired. Ordinary exports and backups remain available and retain saved details; review them before sharing outside StreamLion. Previously shared conversation content remains subject to the receiving service’s policies and your account settings.

Privacy during in-app AI questions

When you choose an optional AI answer, StreamLion reads the selected project and its field notes from your connected Google workbook. On StreamLion’s authenticated Cloudflare service, it filters the question and those records together before sending them to OpenAI. This question filter runs on the service, not in your browser or Google Drive; the service already processes these Google reads. It does not write changes to your Google records or device drafts.

The filter replaces detected names, contact details, street addresses, links and known project identities with temporary reference labels. It excludes the project’s access-instructions field, detected labeled passwords or codes, and recognized repeats of those secrets. Excluded access details are not restored; use saved-detail lookup to read them without sending a question to OpenAI. Filtering can miss unusual formats or identifying context and can withhold wording that appears sensitive. It reduces disclosure rather than guaranteeing anonymity.

Only the filtered question and project context go to OpenAI. The replacement list stays in memory for that request on StreamLion’s service; it is not sent to OpenAI, saved as a Drive file, written to application logs or added to the credit ledger. StreamLion checks the completed model answer before showing text or requesting hosted voice, restores only the references belonging to that request, and keeps the existing answer-size limit after restoration. Unknown or damaged references, an unfinished response or an oversized restored answer fail without delivering unverified text or speech, and reserved credits are returned. There is no automatic model retry.

If you choose hosted spoken playback, DeepInfra receives the restored answer text, which can include names and contact details, to generate speech. Filtering before OpenAI does not hide those restored details from the voice provider. Browser or device speech has its own provider settings. StreamLion requests that OpenAI not save responses as application history; the OpenAI retention explanation below also applies to filtered API questions. Provider policies: OpenAI API data controls and DeepInfra privacy.

StreamLion does not store question audio or AI answer text in the payment ledger, and does not add a durable question/answer recovery copy. Answers can remain in the current browser view until you leave or clear them. Cloudflare retains account-linked balances, payment and request identifiers, times, charges and completion status for payment verification, refunds, capacity limits and retry safety. Pilot grants and test payments stay separate from paid live credits. A completed text answer is charged even if later spoken playback fails.

Downloaded support details contain technical information such as app version, browser, connection status and record counts, excluding project text, workbook identifiers and tokens. You decide whether to send the file. Please do not email passwords, Google tokens or client secrets.

Privacy during optional brief preparation

Optional in-app brief preparation organizes PDF text or pasted messages into a project brief. Before text goes to OpenAI, StreamLion filters detected personal details on the device where the brief is being prepared. This reduces the information OpenAI receives while keeping the resulting brief useful for the job. Manual entry remains available.

  1. Filter on the device. The browser reads the PDF or pasted text. Your original PDF stays on your device. StreamLion replaces detected names, email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses, web links, known project identities and extra phrases you select with temporary reference labels. Detected passwords, access codes and similar secrets are removed instead of replaced for later restoration. The filename is replaced with “Project brief”.
  2. Review before sending. You see the filtered text and can hide more names or phrases before confirming preparation. Automatic detection can miss unusual names, contact formats, unlabeled codes or identifying context. Labels reduce exposure; they do not guarantee anonymity or make confidential material safe to share in every case. Check the preview and remove anything you are not entitled to send.
  3. Prepare, check and correct. OpenAI receives the filtered text, then the prepared brief and any review findings needed to check it against that text. If a problem is found, StreamLion requests one correction and a final check. The replacement list is never sent to OpenAI. All stages use the same displayed credit price, with at most four model requests and no automatic network retries.
  4. Restore useful details. StreamLion’s Cloudflare service receives the filtered text and replacement list separately in the same authenticated request. After AI processing, that service restores the known names, contacts and other replaced details. Removed secrets, including detected access codes, are not restored; check the original access instructions separately. You review the completed brief before adding it to a draft.

Where the information is kept

Filtering runs in your browser, not in Google Drive. The replacement list exists in request memory on StreamLion’s service; it is not sent to OpenAI, written to application logs or saved as a separate file in Drive. The original extracted source text is not automatically added to draft or recovery storage. The restored result, which may contain personal details, is encrypted for recovery by the same account and workspace with the request reference. Recovery does not require the original device’s replacement list.

Results stop being recoverable after 24 hours and are removed by daily cleanup, normally within 48 hours of creation. Account-linked request hashes, charges and completion status remain for refunds, retry safety and spending limits. If processing is interrupted before the encrypted result is saved, recovery does not restart AI processing and stale reservations are returned. Nothing is automatically saved to Google. Once you save the project to Google, its complete details can be read on another connected device. Failed preparations return reserved credits; discarding a completed result does not refund its charge.

What OpenAI may retain

OpenAI does not use API content to train its models by default unless the API customer opts in. StreamLion requests that responses not be saved as application history. This is not a promise of zero retention: OpenAI may retain text and results for safety monitoring, normally for up to 30 days, and longer when required by law or reasonably necessary to prevent harm. See OpenAI’s API data controls. Filtering reduces the personal details in that text; it does not remove OpenAI’s processing or retention obligations.

Optional speech-only clips and voice controls

Device voice and speed preferences stay in this browser. The prerecorded Kokoro publisher sample is served as an ordinary static file and sends no project text to a speech provider. Device/browser voices may use their own speech services.

When available, speech-only clips have their own quoted credit price. StreamLion reads the selected saved project from your authorized Google workbook and shows the exact answer text for review. Only that confirmed text goes to DeepInfra for speech, without an OpenAI request. It can contain names, addresses or contact details. Access instructions and detected secrets are refused; detection can miss unusual formats, so review the text. StreamLion does not save the answer text or generated audio on its servers. DeepInfra’s own data handling terms apply. An encrypted short-lived quote stays in the browser until it is used or expires. Charge/recovery metadata retains a text hash, account/workbook scope hash, request ID, price, provider-cost reservation, time and outcome; it contains no answer text or audio. The browser holds generated audio for the current view and a request recovery reference until resolved.

Client forms, shared briefs and later questions

Filling in a work-order form or using its question-by-question request assistant does not itself send your answers to OpenAI. Your provider receives the submitted request and may choose optional in-app brief preparation when available. The filter described above applies to that preparation step. It does not remove details from the request or shared brief that you and your provider need for the job.

In-app AI questions filter the current question and Google project records again, using the separate service-side process described above. They do not depend on a replacement list from the original brief. Ordinary exports and backups do not apply these filters; review them before sharing outside StreamLion. Initial brief filtering does not hide restored details from other ways you choose to share the project.

6. Retention, disconnecting and deletion

Persistent server sessions expire after up to 90 days and may end earlier if you disconnect or Google revokes authorization. Expired sessions cannot be used; the configured cleanup service removes expired sessions and stale request counters. Request counters older than a day are eligible for cleanup. Infrastructure providers may retain operational logs or backups under their own policies.

Disconnect Google in Connections deletes this browser’s server sign-in session and clears its session cookie. It also revokes background client-coordination grants and their client sessions for that Google account. Revoke client coordination in Client requests ends the selected workbook's grant and associated client sessions. Neither action deletes your local drafts or Google files; other devices have separate Core sign-in sessions. You can revoke StreamLion's Google authorization at Google Account connections.

The retired ChatGPT integration no longer accepts connection grants, tokens or project requests. Existing extension grants expire after at most 30 days or when their Google session ends. Temporary extension review copies expire after 24 hours and remain eligible for daily cleanup, normally within 48 hours of creation. Expired or revoked connection records remain eligible for cleanup. Previously sent ChatGPT attachments and tool results remain subject to your OpenAI account settings; retiring StreamLion access does not erase earlier conversation messages.

To remove Google records, delete the relevant files or rows in Sheets and Drive, including their trash where applicable. To remove device records, remove the offline workbook copy in Connections and clear StreamLion site storage after checking your backup. Project archive actions are not a promise of permanent erasure. Contact us to request removal of server connection records associated with your account.

Coordination access expires on the recorded job deadline; original Google records and Drive archive packages remain under the provider’s control. Client push subscriptions end with their access grant, and provider subscriptions last at most 90 days before renewal. Logout/unsubscribe removes the matching subscription; maintenance removes expired subscriptions and old push queue rows. Accepted/skipped email queue content is replaced with a minimal receipt. Signed webhook metadata is eligible for removal after seven days. Undelivered messages may be retained for investigation. Resend and device push providers retain their own service records under their policies; deleting StreamLion records does not erase previously delivered email or downloaded files.

A provider may explicitly archive a closed or cancelled job before its normal access deadline, ending current client access. A verified archive retains job history and references to app-owned originals in the provider's private Drive folder; those originals must remain available for recovery. External reference links do not back up the linked files. Recovering an archive into another authorized workbook preserves the source files and history and does not restore an expired client session. Reopening an archived job requires fresh client verification.

Purchase, refund and dispute records are retained as needed to provide purchased access and meet accounting, tax, security and legal obligations. Disconnecting Google removes the browser connection, not the purchase record. Contact us for purchase-data requests; required financial records may be retained. A short-lived device receipt permits previously verified purchasers to work locally offline for up to seven days; it grants no server or Google access.

Support and launch-interest correspondence is retained while needed to respond, resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Ask us to delete it or stop launch messages. A deletion request does not erase copies you or third parties have stored independently.

7. Privacy requests and your responsibilities

For provider-controlled client and project records, our processing follows the provider's authorized service instructions and the applicable customer agreement. Business customers can contact us about data-processing terms and international-transfer arrangements before using the service with client records. Google-owned files and privacy filtering do not, by themselves, establish a legal transfer safeguard or eliminate our processing obligations.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local privacy regulator. Email info@transcendencemedia.com with your request and the Google email associated with StreamLion, where relevant. We may verify your identity before acting. We will respond as required by applicable law.

For Google connection features, processing supports the service you request and the authorization you grant. Security processing supports protection of the service; other processing may be required by law. Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it. Google file rights can also be exercised directly through your Google account.

You control the client, contact and site information you add. Obtain any necessary permissions and avoid placing sensitive information in records when it is not needed for the job.

8. Children and changes to this policy

StreamLion is a professional tool intended for adults, not a service directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

We will update the effective date when this policy changes. For material changes we will provide notice through the app or another appropriate channel. If a change requires consent, including a new use of Google data, we will seek it before that use begins.

Account activity and owner administration

StreamLion retains operational account metadata, including your verified Google account identity, first-known/sign-in dates, payment and credit records, and content-free activity dates. Foreground-use signals and completed server actions support account-use reporting. Offline activity signals are queued on your device for at most seven days and sent only for the same signed-in account; they never determine billing or access. Daily activity metadata is retained for 90 days. Financial records, known-account metadata and immutable administrative receipts follow the operator's record-retention policy.

The restricted owner portal reports account identity, purchases, credit balances and usage without opening your Google files or exposing OAuth credentials. Complimentary admin credits and corrections are recorded with a reason and audit receipt. Unused admin goodwill survives a Core refund, but remains subject to the account's existing access rules and feature availability. Administrative reports are not cached for offline use.
