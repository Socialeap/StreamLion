How can we help?

StreamLion brings project details, room readings, field notes and handovers together for spatial capture providers.

Email info@transcendencemedia.com for app, Google connection, in-app AI or purchase help.

Help us understand the problem

Tell us what you were trying to do, what happened, and which StreamLion page you were using. Include your device and browser or app version if known.

You may attach a screenshot after hiding private project details. Please do not send passwords, authorization tokens, client secrets or full payment card details.

Connection or save problems

Open Connections in the StreamLion web app to check your Google account and selected workbook. If a save is interrupted, keep the draft and check its status before retrying. Download a device backup before clearing browser storage.

The former StreamLion ChatGPT integration is retired. Use the StreamLion workspace for project work.

Purchases and refunds

For a purchase or refund request, include your purchase receipt. Our 14-day money-back guarantee covers requests within 14 days of purchase and before your first project delivery through StreamLion. Earlier purchase terms and mandatory consumer rights are preserved. Credit refund requests are reviewed separately.

Privacy and your data

Saved project records remain in your Google workbook and Drive. For access, correction or deletion requests, contact us and see the privacy policy.
