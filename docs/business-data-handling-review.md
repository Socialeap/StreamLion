## Actual roles and processing to cover

Transcendence Media LLC operates StreamLion. It determines account, billing, fraud-prevention and support processing purposes. For provider-controlled client/project records, document the applicable controller/processor roles and instructions in the customer agreement; choosing Google-owned storage does not remove StreamLion's processing of authenticated reads, writes and coordination queues.

The DPA for review should cover subject matter/duration, project/contact categories, provider instructions and authorization, confidentiality, existing encryption/access controls, subprocessors, breach notification without undue delay, assistance with rights/security obligations, audits, and deletion/return subject to required financial retention. Describe actual operations; do not promise a SOC2 certification, EU-only hosting, instant deletion of vendor backups or capabilities not implemented.

Identify Cloudflare, Google and Resend processing and applicable vendor agreements/locations. Stripe handles payment/business tax data under its applicable roles and terms.
