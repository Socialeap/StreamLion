## Evidence packet

Use the existing private support process, never a public GitHub issue, for real customer evidence. Record:

- Core order/receipt, authenticated immutable Google subject and payment mode. Do not match solely by current email or display name.
- Terms actually offered at purchase and their effective date; earlier buyers retain more favourable promises. Do not apply the first-delivery exclusion retrospectively.
- Purchase time, refund-request receipt time and the applicable deadline, with time zones.
- Earliest **verified successful delivery** across relevant projects, if any: signed events, Completion Brief/file reference and hash, completed coordination journal and verification outcome. Scope approval, drafting, failed/pending delivery and client acknowledgment are not substitutes.
- Evidence sufficient to distinguish the initial plan time from successful retry completion. Reopening can clear the current deliveredAt field. Current job state or a planned timestamp alone cannot establish first delivery.
- Reviewer, evidence reviewed, eligibility reasoning, unresolved questions and decision. Consider preserved mandatory rights and applicable exceptions; do not automatically deny because evidence is missing.

Preserve the reviewed evidence packet in the existing private support records when needed. Completed coordination journals are cleaned after30days; the live application is not a permanent refund-evidence archive. This checklist adds no new collection system or retention policy.
