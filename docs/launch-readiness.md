## Product limits and unfinished planned features

The first release supports one provider, one verified commissioning client, one primary site, and explicit appointment/fee/delivery agreement. Provider-reported payment is labelled; bank reconciliation is not connected. Clients never trigger AI spend. Only fully verified mutual agreement activates the one-time project charge.

The committed pilot limits remain: 100 open requests per workbook, a 10,000-total-grid-row/tab adapter ceiling, 512 KiB PDF/JPEG/PNG uploads with at most 20 attachments per job, and a bounded encrypted archive. These are safety limits, not proven throughput or storage guarantees.

Provider-versioned service/intake templates and conditional questions are implemented in the separate [service-template candidate](service-intake-templates.md), with saved versions pinned to new requests and required answers enforced during agreement. PR #65 is open and its required checks passed at `b49d4abc5121fb38c56c80e3eba3461b4d3b8433`; owner merge approval, Cloudflare Functions/frontend deployment and fresh authorized Google acceptance remain open. PR #64's client-link fixes are also review-ready with passing required checks at `22fab280f670f528b3c84d7dc3b24acd6695e320`.

The separate [archive candidate](archive-recovery.md) implements signed version 2 packages, exact original-file verification, atomic cold restoration into another authorized workbook, source/destination recovery gates and a non-destructive workbook rollover procedure. A reasoned **Archive now** action for closed jobs ends client access explicitly and preserves the exact final history without changing production clocks. The full candidate suite passes 444 tests, including actual Workers bounded-read checks; app/extension builds and Functions compilation pass. Native local UI checks are synthetic. Required CI, concrete merge approval, deployment and live synthetic Google archive/restore proof are still separate gates.

Linked multi-location requests and agency roles are later increments under the MVP spec. Automatic destructive archive compaction and a full operator/cost dashboard remain unfinished. Rollover retains original books/files and bounds the new active workbook; it does not shrink or silently clear old history. Retained history is never deleted to make a capacity test pass. Their intended release scope must stay visible; no public page should advertise an undeployed or unverified capability as delivered.
