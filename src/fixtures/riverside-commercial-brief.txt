Subject: Capture scope — Riverside Business Centre, Levels 1 and 2

Hi,

Following our call, we need a current 3D record of the occupied office space before the refurbishment starts. The tour will support discussions between our facilities team, architect and fit-out contractor. Please review the scope below and flag anything that needs a separate visit or additional pricing.

We are provisionally looking at next Tuesday morning. Building management has not confirmed access yet, so please do not treat that as a booked appointment.

LEVEL 1 — PUBLIC AND SHARED AREAS

Please scan the entrance vestibule, reception, visitor waiting area, main corridor and lift lobby. Include the connection between reception and the rear loading corridor so the contractor can understand the delivery route.

Photograph the reception desk, visitor signage, existing floor finishes and any visible damage along that route. We do not need detailed photographs of every decorative item.

Measure the clear width and height of the main entrance doorway and the doorway between reception and the loading corridor. We also need the loading corridor's narrowest clear width. Please distinguish the available clearance from the overall wall-to-wall dimension.

LEVEL 1 — OFFICE SUITE

Capture the open-plan workspace, both meeting rooms, the kitchenette, print area and accessible toilet. The two meeting rooms are labelled Cedar and Maple on the doors; please use those names consistently.

For Cedar and Maple, record room length, width and ceiling height. In the kitchenette, measure the available wall length behind the existing units and the clear opening into the room.

The open-plan area contains occupied desks. Please coordinate with our site contact before capturing people, personal paperwork or computer screens. We can arrange a short break for staff if necessary.

LEVEL 2 — PROPOSED EXPANSION SPACE

Please capture the vacant office suite, breakout area and corridor connecting them to the stair landing. Include the internal stair connection between the two levels if building management permits access.

Measure the vacant suite's overall length and width, plus ceiling height at the entrance and at the rear. There appears to be a ceiling step near the rear windows, so please identify the locations of those readings rather than giving us one general height.

We also need photographs showing the columns, existing floor boxes, exposed services and the junction between the vacant suite and breakout area.

EXCLUSIONS AND CONDITIONAL ACCESS

The server room and electrical cupboard are outside this scope. Do not enter or scan either space.

The records store is normally locked. Include it only if our facilities manager confirms that it has been cleared and authorizes access on the day. Otherwise, identify it as inaccessible and tell us what would be required for a return visit.

Please do not enter the roof, remove ceiling tiles, open service panels or move furniture. Any concealed conditions should be recorded as unknown rather than inferred.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU ATTEND

Can you confirm whether the loading corridor and internal stair can be included in the same navigable tour, or whether they would need separate capture sections?

Our architect has also asked for "window dimensions" in the vacant suite. We have not established whether that means the glazing, frame or structural opening. Please clarify this with us before taking measurements.

The proposed new partition positions are not final. Please document existing conditions only; do not mark proposed dimensions as verified site measurements.

DELIVERY

Please provide a private tour link, photographs labelled by level and area, and a measurement schedule showing each location and dimension. Keep approximate observations separate from checked measurements.

Include a short exceptions list covering inaccessible areas, anything omitted, and questions that remain unresolved. Let us know whether a floor-plan export is included in your quotation or would be an additional service.

We would like a draft by Thursday afternoon, subject to the visit being confirmed for Tuesday. Please send the draft to our project manager for review before sharing it with the architect or contractor. The tour should not be publicly listed.
