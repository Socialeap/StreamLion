A few things worth knowing.

Does it replace my capture platform?

No. StreamLion organizes the work around your capture. Use it alongside your preferred 3D or 360 platform. It does not create scans, host tours or require a particular camera.

Do I need ChatGPT?

No. Project preparation, field records, measurements and checklists work without it. If you want help with a brief or a question, you can copy a dated project snapshot into your own ChatGPT account. That is not live access to your workbook.

Where does my project data live?

Google projects and files stay in your chosen Google Sheets workbook and Drive. Device drafts and optional offline copies stay in your browser. StreamLion stores encrypted Google authorization on Cloudflare so a valid connection can reopen. You can disconnect and export your device records.

Are updates included?

Updates to the purchased app arrive automatically; you may be asked to reload when it is safe. New features are in development. Pricing and availability of future premium upgrades will be stated separately—this purchase does not promise every future product or upgrade.
