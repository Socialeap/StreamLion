      Quick answers.

      Can I plan a project before choosing a provider?

      Yes. Plan your capture project for free in your browser, with no signup or AI. Your draft stays in browser memory unless you explicitly save on this device or download a copy, copy reviewed text, or create a private invitation online. No exact address or access codes are needed. To store a private invitation on F3D, sign in free and review the exact snapshot and selected recipient. Invite a provider by email or choose an opted-in public listing; the provider must verify their email and enroll free on F3D before opening or replying. No Core purchase is required for this F3D participation. You explicitly copy and send the invitation link; no email is sent automatically. Listings and responses do not establish a signed scope or reserve availability.

      Private access expires after seven days. Clients can revoke access or delete the stored brief and reply. Expired records are removed by an hourly scheduled purge; minimal abuse-prevention metadata is kept for up to seven days after deletion. Downloaded or shared copies cannot be revoked. Editing a local draft does not change an existing invitation: revoke it and create a fresh snapshot. On another device, providers should reopen the original invitation link.

      A provider can open the downloaded JSON in StreamLion using Import saved project details. It becomes a local draft to review together, not an approved scope. Importing does not automatically write to Google; the provider reviews and saves the project separately.

      How does a client find me and start a project?

      Share your branded request link directly when client coordination is available. It introduces your business and chosen service before intake. Private invitations verify the client’s email; public forms can require email confirmation too. Clients need no app installation, Core purchase or credit wallet. Check the coordination availability notice before inviting a client.

      Can I start with a client’s PDF brief?

      Yes. Import a readable PDF or text file, or paste the requested work. Review suggested tasks beside their original passages and page references, then edit or add anything needed. For a scanned document, paste the readable wording instead.

      What goes into the Completion Brief?

      A handover under your provider name: requested work, task records, reviewed room readings, photos, exceptions, references and next actions. StreamLion assembles it from your project records. Preview it, choose the records to include, then print or save a PDF and share it with your client.

      Can I keep internal notes out of the report?

      Yes. Unchecked field records and separate payment fields are excluded by default. Review the preview and your source wording before sharing; free-text passages remain as recorded.

      Can my client see progress and clarify something during the visit?

      Yes, when client coordination is available. Keep questions, agreed changes, saved progress and delivery links in the project portal. Both parties need a connection for new messages and updates; the client sees shared updates after saving and refresh. Your private field records are not automatically shared.

      When are the 240 project credits used?

      For newly agreed jobs, review the Completion Brief and confirm delivery to the client portal. That delivery uses the quoted 240 project credits once. A preview uses no project credits. If your balance is too low, your work is kept while you add credits and retry. Jobs already charged under the earlier approval-based model are not charged again. AI is quoted and charged separately.

      What happens if reception drops at the site?

      Keep working with available project details and device drafts. Pending changes remain marked until you can send them to Google. Confirm the saved status before treating the Google workbook as up to date, and use device backups to protect local work.

      Does it replace my capture platform?

      No. Keep your camera and capture platform. StreamLion organizes the job; it does not scan spaces or host tours.

      Can I ask about a project by voice?

      Yes. Open a saved project and tap Ask by voice for supported details such as contact, access or deliverables. Answers show saved-field sources; missing details stay missing. Optional read-aloud and speech input depend on your browser. Typing works too. No AI API key is needed.

      How does the project assistant work?

      Ask works inside StreamLion. Lookup of supported saved project details is included, with optional device or browser read-aloud. Broader AI answers and AI brief preparation use prepaid StreamLion credits when available, with the price shown before use. Manual entry, ordinary exports and backups remain available without AI credits.

      Where does my project data live?

      Saved projects stay in your chosen Google Sheets and Drive. Drafts can stay on this device; a secure sign-in connection helps reopen your workspace. How your data is handled.

      Are updates included?

      App updates arrive automatically. Future premium upgrades may be priced separately; their terms will be clear before purchase.
